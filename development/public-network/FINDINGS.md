# Unresolved findings and public limitations

This is the unresolved-finding ledger and the public-limitations list that gate Q1 (release review) of
[PLAN.md](PLAN.md) requires. It records, with their scope and sources, the open findings, the stated limits of the
design and the implementation, and the risks the owner has accepted for PoCol Mainnet and its rehearsal network.

- **Q1 stays open.** By owner decision of 2026-11-18 (decision 7) the owner commissions an independent security review
  after the launch ([OWNER-DECISIONS-2026-11-18.md](../decisions/OWNER-DECISIONS-2026-11-18.md)). Writing this ledger
  is one Q1 deliverable; it does not close Q1 or any other gate.
- **A gate closes only with its evidence.** A gate of the acceptance ledger closes only with its implementation and
  evidence, never by changing a status label.
- **The network launches with these entries open,** as PLAN.md's launch order says. The node's readiness report
  (`publicLaunchReady`, `launchBlockers`) is node-local and refuses nothing: nodes run, mine and serve sites
  regardless.

Recorded on 2026-11-20 against `main` at 8b465b9. Line numbers in the sources refer to that tree. The ledger has
91 entries: 35 open findings, 43 limitations and 13 accepted risks.

## How to read the ledger

- **Kind.** `open-finding`: a defect, gap or inaccurate text with a fix still to be made. `limitation`: a stated limit
  of the design, the implementation or the evidence. `accepted-risk`: a risk the owner decided to accept or defer, with
  the decision cited.
- **Severity.** `high`, `medium`, `low` or `info`: how much the entry matters to someone who holds value on, mines on or
  operates the network. It is not a launch condition.
- **Gate.** The acceptance-ledger gate of [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) the entry belongs to (K1, J1, I1, A1, S1, N1, L1, E1, Q1),
  or `-` when it belongs to none.
- **Sources.** Repository paths linked relative to this file (with line numbers where a passage is cited; a few
  sources are a whole file or directory), a git log reference (F-03) and two GitHub Actions runs (F-51). Each statement
  says only what its sources show.

## Public limitations

In plain words, for people who use, mine on or operate PoCol Mainnet:

- **No independent security review yet.** No independent external human security audit has been done; the reviews so
  far are automated or AI-agent reviews. The owner commissions an independent review after the launch. (F-01, F-02, F-03)
- **Security is not proven.** PoW-equivalent security, fairness and Sybil resistance are design objectives, not proven
  properties. Registration with a zero bond is a measured experiment. (F-04, F-29, F-30, F-32)
- **Do not use PREVRANDAO as fair randomness.** Block producers can bias it; it is unsuitable for lotteries or fair
  selection. (F-06)
- **Finality is local.** `finalized` and `safe` are each node's own local floors, not committee or mathematical
  finality. Wait for confirmations that match the value at stake. If a node's own side of a partition extends its
  branch 2,048 blocks (about 5.7 hours) or more past the fork, that node stays on its branch and needs a resync or a
  manual checkpoint; a node that was only offline syncs normally when it reconnects. (F-08, F-09)
- **The software reports open gates.** Nodes report `publicLaunchReady: false` and list what is not yet established;
  the network is admitted in `ExperimentalDevnet` scope as a Simnet profile. This report stops nothing. (F-10)
- **Execution and memory bounds are measured, not proven worst cases.** Block timing, gas and memory bounds rest on
  measurements, models and stated assumptions; some RPC resource guards of the specification are not enforced. A node's
  native RPC binds loopback only; public RPC goes through a rate-limited gateway. (F-11, F-12, F-13, F-14, F-15, F-21, F-22, F-23)
- **Disk use grows without limit.** The block journal is never compacted: in the worst case about 1.7 GB a day per node.
  (F-26)
- **Fees can be lost on cap halts.** A transaction that hits a block or state cap pays its full declared gas. The state
  caps can be filled for ordinary gas; once the account cap is full, newcomers without an account cannot register until
  a scheduled cap-raise fork. (F-18, F-19)
- **Seats at a full set.** At 10,000 members an inactive member, a founder included, can be replaced after about an
  hour without a credited share. (F-31)
- **The owner holds all founders and the initial supply.** At genesis the four founders and the 4,000,000 PCOL supply
  are the owner's. (F-34)
- **Peer connections are not encrypted.** The P2P transport is plain TCP with self-declared identities; the three
  founder servers are the first seeds. (F-35, F-36, F-38)
- **Keys are your responsibility.** Key files on Linux and Android are plaintext at rest; there is no key rotation in
  the Registry and no passphrase reset; uninstalling the Android app erases its key. Keep an encrypted backup and its
  passphrase. (F-42, F-47, F-48)
- **Verify downloads with the signed SHA256SUMS.** A matching hash alone does not authenticate the publisher; releases
  are signed by one owner-held key. Only the Linux release binaries and package and the extension zip have a CI double
  build (two builds on one CI image); the Windows package and the APK have none, and nobody has independently rebuilt a
  release. (F-51, F-52, F-53)
- **The browser extension trusts its remote node** and installs in Chrome's Developer mode, not from the Chrome Web
  Store. (F-67, F-69)
- **Public services have single points.** One gateway host serves the explorer, public RPC, the Studio relay and the
  fee faucet. The faucet gives 0.002 PCOL once per new address from a hot key, and refuses every claim while that key's account
  holds more than 10 PCOL. (F-41, F-71, F-72)
- **The rehearsal measurements are still to be taken.** The profile values are decided, not yet measured on the
  founders' hosts. (F-77)

## Adding and closing entries

- **Adding.** A new entry takes the next unused id (`F-NN`; ids are never reused or renumbered), an area, a kind, a
  severity, a statement that says only what its sources show, the gate it belongs to (or `-`) and its sources as
  linked repository paths, with line numbers where a passage is cited. A duplicate of an existing entry is merged into it, not added.
- **Closing.** An entry is closed only with the change that fixes it and its evidence (a merged commit or pull request,
  and a test, measurement or record that shows the fix). A closed entry stays in the table: after its title its statement
  begins with "Closed:" and points to the fixing change and its evidence. A changed status label alone closes nothing.
- **Gates.** Closing entries does not close a gate: a gate of [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) closes only when the acceptance
  ledger marks it closed with its own evidence, and `prototypes/pocold/src/readiness.rs` (`PLAN_GATES_OPEN`) follows
  that ledger.
- **Checks.** `deploy/websites/tests/test_launch_decisions.py` (class `FindingsLedger`) checks that every relative link
  and cited path in this file exists, that every cited line number is no larger than its file's line count, that every
  `F-NN` id is unique and in order, and that none of a fixed list of phrases appears that call the launch not ready,
