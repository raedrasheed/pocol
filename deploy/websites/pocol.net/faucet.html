How the faucet works

  • 0.002 PCOL, once per address, to pay the fee of the first registration.
  • New accounts only: the address must not have sent a transaction, must not be a contract and must not already hold that much PCOL.
  • Daily limits apply: a total number of transfers each UTC day for everyone, and a smaller number per connection.
  • The faucet is not the only way: any PCOL holder can send you the fee with an ordinary transfer.
  • This page sends only your address to explorer.pocol.net, which runs the faucet.
